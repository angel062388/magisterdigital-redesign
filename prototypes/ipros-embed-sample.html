Three things to know before you paste this into a page

  • Page brands itself as "Quantum Agency" in the top-left. Reads as a partner, not Magister. Ask InstantProspector to swap the logo before ship, or embed as-is and treat it as a co-brand.
  • Full page is 2,859px tall, iframe height is 900px, so visitors will scroll inside the frame while the outer Magister page stays still. Consider height="2900" to remove the inner scrollbar, or trim to a shorter above-the-fold CTA.
  • frameborder="0" is deprecated in HTML5 (harmless, browsers ignore it). Your border-radius:12px is what actually rounds the corners.
