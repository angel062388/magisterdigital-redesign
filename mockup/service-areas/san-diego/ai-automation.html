02 · The San Diego intake reality

AI intake in San Diego is a corridor problem, not a citywide one.

San Diego is the second largest metro in California, 3.3 million people spread across half a dozen cities in one media market. A missed call does not cost the same in every one of them. A plumber covering North County, Poway and La Mesa is fielding emergency calls across a service area too wide for one on-call phone. A practice near Torrey Pines, UCSD and La Jolla is qualifying consult inquiries the moment they arrive, not the next morning. A firm taking case calls downtown and in Chula Vista is triaging leads that land from two ends of the county inside the same hour.

The corridors that price the highest make the miss the most expensive. Torrey Pines and downtown are among the most competitive corridors in the state, and more than 1,400 life science firms keep inquiry volume high around La Jolla and UCSD alone. We read your intake before we build anything: where your calls come from, what hour they arrive, and what a missed one is costing you in booked work.

07 · Questions San Diego operators ask first

The questions a careful San Diego buyer is already holding

What happens to the automation when the engagement ends?
The agents, workflows, training data, and log structure belong to your business. They live in your CRM and your infrastructure. You do not lose access to anything when we stop working together.
Does the intake agent cover after-hours calls across San Diego's zones?
Yes. The agent triggers the moment an inquiry arrives, whether that is a plumber's emergency call from North County or Poway at 11pm, a consult request from Torrey Pines or La Jolla, or a case inquiry from downtown or Chula Vista. It qualifies on your team's own logic, books the slot or hands off to a named person on your team, and writes every decision to an audit row your office manager can read the next morning.
Can we meet at the Pacific Beach office?
Yes, that can be arranged. The founders and the in-house team work out of 1135 Garnet Ave #13 in Pacific Beach, and San Diego is the one market where a working session can happen in a room. Most sessions still run on video with the dashboard on screen, because that is where your numbers live, and every other market we serve runs the same way.
How fast can an AI intake agent go live for a San Diego business?
The audit comes first. We walk your intake process and your team's actual decision logic, then hand you a written spec of three builds ranked by estimated revenue impact, built inside the CRM you already run rather than a new platform. If the math does not work for your business right now, we say so on the call and you keep your money. The build only starts once you have approved the spec.
What if the agent makes a mistake?
We monitor the log weekly. When the agent misqualifies a lead or drafts a quote with the wrong labor rate, we flag it, adjust the logic, and document the change. You see the adjustment in the log. We do not wait for you to notice a pattern and file a support ticket.
Does AI automation work for medical practices under HIPAA?
It can, within defined limits. We do not automate exchanges that involve protected health information without a formal HIPAA-compliant infrastructure review. We do automate consultation booking, follow-up on general inquiries, and content pipelines inside marketing copy. We name exactly where the compliance line sits for your practice at the audit stage.
How much does AI automation cost?
AI automation as a single-channel engagement starts at $20K per month. Full-stack engagements that include AI automation, SEO, paid media, and CRM start at $60K per month. We scope from there based on the number of builds, your CRM environment, and how much of the operation we run. The audit produces a specific number before you commit.
