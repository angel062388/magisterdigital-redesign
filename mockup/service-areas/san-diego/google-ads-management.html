07 · Questions San Diego operators ask first

The questions a careful San Diego buyer is already holding

What should we budget for Google Ads management in San Diego?
Single-channel Google Ads engagements start at $20K per month. Full-stack engagements including SEO, AI automation, and CRM start at $60K per month. We scope from there based on market intensity, campaign volume, and how much of the attribution architecture we build from scratch. Torrey Pines and downtown are among the most competitive corridors in the state, so San Diego accounts tend to scope toward the higher end of that range.
Do you manage Performance Max?
Yes, with proper feeds, asset groups, audience signals from your first-party CRM data, and brand exclusions. We do not hand Performance Max the keys and call it strategy.
How do you connect Google Ads to our CRM?
We build the GA4 to CRM import in Week 2. HubSpot, Go High Level, ServiceTitan, Salesforce, and Jobber all integrate differently. Once live, leads from every campaign carry a source tag through your pipeline to closed-won.
Do you run Demand Gen and YouTube?
Both are part of the stack where the sales cycle runs longer than two weeks or consumer awareness is the bottleneck. We scope those channels into your 90-day plan after Month 1 attribution is clean, not before.
Who actually runs the account?
Dimitry Morgan, Head of Paid Media at Magister Digital, operating with ties to Denver Digital Agency and Green Hat Local SEO. The same operator who builds your account reads your brief every Monday. No account coordinators, no junior managers running your spend, no handoff after the contract is signed.
How competitive is Google Ads in San Diego?
Torrey Pines and downtown are among the most competitive corridors in the state, and more than 1,400 life science firms alone compete for space in that corridor before an ad ever earns a click. Run this test: open your current report and find cost per closed deal, not cost per lead, for your San Diego campaigns specifically. If that number is not there, the account was never built for how competitive this market actually is.
Can we meet at the Pacific Beach office?
Yes, that can be arranged. The founders and the in-house team work out of 1135 Garnet Ave #13 in Pacific Beach, and San Diego is the one market where a working session can happen in a room. Most sessions still run on video with the dashboard on screen, because that is where your numbers live, and every other market we serve runs the same way.
