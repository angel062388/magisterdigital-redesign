07 · Questions operators ask first

The questions a careful buyer is already holding.

What happens to the automation when the engagement ends?
The agents, workflows, training data, and log structure belong to your business. They live in your CRM and your infrastructure. You do not lose access to anything when we stop working together.
How is this different from buying Go High Level or another platform?
A platform gives you a canvas. We build the system that sits on it. Whether your stack is Go High Level, HubSpot, ServiceTitan, or a bespoke build, we spec the workflows against your actual intake data and deploy inside the tools your team already uses. A system is not a platform.
Can my team see what the agent is doing?
Yes. Every decision writes an audit row with the inputs, the rule that fired, the action, and the outcome. Your team reads the log the same way they read any other queue. No vendor permission needed to see any of it.
What if the agent makes a mistake?
Weekly log review flags misqualifications and confidence-band anomalies. Rule adjustments and re-tuning happen in the same session, and every change is documented in the log. You are not waiting for a support ticket.
Does AI automation work for medical practices under HIPAA?
Within limits. No PHI in automated exchanges. Booking, follow-up, and post-visit reminders are safe. The audit call defines the exact line for your practice before anything ships.
How much does AI automation cost?
Single-channel starts at $20,000 per month. Full-stack, multi-workflow deployments start at $60,000 per month. The written spec that comes out of the audit call produces your exact number, not a range.
