04 · The diagnosis

The map and the phone, not the website

01How injury patients actually search

Injury patients do not research chiropractors the way marketers assume. In the hours after a crash they search on a phone, look at the map results, and call whoever looks credible and close.

02The website is a bystander

Over this same window, visits to the site fell about a third while calls from the map listings more than doubled. The behavior is the diagnosis: patients call from the listing without ever opening the site.

03So the work went where the patients are

The layer that produces patients is the map pack and the phone, so that is where the work went.

05 · The build

Three listings, three separate map races

What we did, in the order it mattered. The success measure was set up front, and it was not a marketing stat: more patients on the schedule. The first formal review of that measure is this page, dated August 17, 2026.

01Rebuilt the local identity around the search

The practice now operates on an exact-match local domain built around “car crash doctor,” a term patients type in the hours after an accident. That term and its “near me” variant now hold the top map spot in two of the four tracked areas. Part of that position is the domain itself, and we say so, because that was the point of the rebrand.

02Worked each listing as its own market

Three clinics means three separate local map races, not one. Each profile was built out and maintained on its own, and the practice's brand name now holds the top spot in three of the four tracked areas, with “car accident chiropractor near me,” searched 4,400 times a month in this metro, on page one in two of them.

03Cleaned the data before claiming anything

The call figures count first-time callers only, exclude repeat callers, and exclude a robocall burst we identified and removed. No growth number on this page rests on one system: our call tracking and Google's own listing reports show the same curve independently.

04The measure that matters, set up front

Not a marketing stat: more patients on the schedule. That stage of the funnel stays with the practice, which is why the patient and revenue rows below are marked pending rather than estimated.

05Did not do

Did not buy the growth: no big ad budget sits behind these figures. Did not estimate what the practice keeps internal: patient and revenue rows stay pending. Did not let the owner's words become a metric: his report of more patients appears only as his own quote.

08 · What went sideways

Traffic fell, and that is the mechanism

Website traffic fell while the phone got busier: site visits dropped about a third over the window, and broad city-wide search rankings declined too. We report that instead of hiding it, because it is the mechanism at work. Injury patients call from the map listing without ever visiting the site. If we had graded this engagement on traffic, it would look like a failure. Graded on patient calls, it is the opposite.

“I'm getting more phone calls and more patients. I'm busier now than I was this time last year.”

Practice owner, auto-injury chiropractic group · shared verbally · anecdotal, never a metric on this page

How the counting worked, and what it cannot see

  • 01A first-time patient call is a conversation from a caller number not previously seen in the tracking history. Repeat callers are excluded
  • 02A 42-call robocall burst in May 2026 was identified by sequential caller numbers, generic caller IDs, and uniform 31–33 second durations, and removed from all figures; May's raw total of 61 becomes a clean 19. January is excluded from every claim because tracking went live January 14
  • 03Call sourcing comes from CallRail tracking-number attribution, with Google Business Profile reporting as an independent check on the same behavior. What this data cannot see: which callers booked, showed, and became patients. The practice keeps those figures internal, so no number for that stage appears on this page, and none is estimated
  • 04Cost basis: full investment for the window, agency fee, media, software, production, and internal labor, is pending. No return-on-investment figure is claimed, because a return cannot be computed honestly without the practice's revenue figures. The engagement did not lean on a large paid-media budget; the exact media figure will be stated when the cost basis is published
  • 05Outside factors: monthly call volume at this scale is volatile, and June dipped before July returned to the high. That is why the two-month check appears above. No algorithm update or competitor event was identified as driving the change, and no unusually large single case sits inside these figures

What this proves, and what it does not

Calls are not patients: the practice does not share patient or revenue figures with us, and the owner's report of more patients is anecdotal and appears only as his own quote, never as a metric. This page proves demand growth at the phone, not revenue. The top map positions on “crash doctor” terms are partly driven by the exact-match domain, a tactic that transfers only to a business willing to rebrand around a search phrase. Broad city-wide search rankings and website traffic declined over this window, one of the three locations lags the others on non-brand terms, and monthly volumes at this scale swing enough that single-month comparisons should be read alongside the two-month check. The result depends on a market where injury patients choose from map results; it will not transfer to a business whose buyers research on websites before contacting anyone.
