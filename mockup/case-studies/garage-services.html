02 · The situation

Two questions on the table, in this order

Storage and organization systems for garages, four US metros, roughly $250,000 in H1 media across Google and Meta. The client asked is the money working, and does the reporting explain what's happening.

We spent the first two weeks reading the account without touching a lever, on the theory that “cut Meta” is a fast answer if it is the right one and a very expensive answer if it is not.

03 · The diagnosis

The dashboard and the account disagreed at three points

Each item on the left is what the internal dashboard read. Each item on the right is what the raw account said once we compared it against what the platforms reported directly.

What they thought
  • 01Google Ads was doing the heavy lifting.
  • 02Meta was underperforming and worth cutting.
  • 03The reporting dashboard was reading the numbers correctly.
What we found
  • 01Google Ads was healthy, but conversion accounting was double-counting.
  • 02Meta was misconfigured. CBO was pulling budget out of tests before they finished.
  • 03CallRail marked leads as “qualified” by hand from sales notes, not by source, so quality-per-channel was invisible.
