09 / QUESTIONS

The things people ask before booking

What does this cost?
Engagements are scoped on the call against your close rate and average ticket, rather than from a rate card. If the numbers do not support the spend we will say so on that call rather than three months in.
Do you guarantee a cost per lead?
No, and be careful with anyone who does before seeing your intake. A guaranteed cost per lead is easy to hit by buying cheaper, lower-intent volume, which is exactly the outcome you are trying to avoid.
How long before anything moves?
Paid channels can move inside the first month. Organic and local visibility are slower and depend on where you start. We set that expectation on the call from your baseline, not an industry average.
Do we have to switch our CRM or website?
Usually not. We instrument what you have first. If something genuinely blocks measurement we will show you what it is costing before recommending a change.
Who actually does the work?
The founders and the in-house team. Strategy is not handed to a junior after the sale, and there is no account manager relaying messages.
What if we are outside your three verticals?
We refer you to someone who works in your category. Learning an unfamiliar industry on a client's budget wastes a year for both sides.

Bring your numbers. We will tell you what they say.
