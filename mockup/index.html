We would rather you self-select than sit through a pitch

Most agencies will take any account that clears the invoice. We would rather tell you now if this is not going to work, so read both columns honestly.

This works if you

  • →Run a home services, medical or dental, or legal business doing real volume already.
  • →Can answer the phone. Demand generation is wasted on a business that cannot pick up.
  • →Will let us instrument the whole funnel, including calls and the CRM, not just the ad platform.
  • →Are willing to be measured on booked work rather than on rankings and impressions.

This does not work if you

  • ×Need leads this week. Nothing honest moves that fast.

The strategy call

What lands on the call, whether or not you hire us

Forty-five minutes. No deck. You leave with the findings either way.

  1. Where your current spend leaks

    A read on the accounts you are already running, and the specific line items that are buying volume rather than work.

  2. Your real cost per booked job

    Not cost per lead. We work back from close rate and average ticket, using your numbers, so the figure survives contact with your P&L.

  3. The intake gap

    Where enquiries are being lost between the click and the calendar. This is usually the cheapest fix on the list.

  4. What we would do in the first ninety days

    Sequenced, with the reasoning attached, so you can hand it to another agency if you would rather.

Questions

The things people ask before booking

What does this cost?
Engagements are scoped on the call, against your close rate and average ticket rather than a rate card. If the numbers do not support the spend we will say so on that call rather than three months in.
Do you guarantee a cost per lead?
No, and be careful with anyone who does before they have seen your intake. A guaranteed cost per lead is easy to hit by buying cheaper, lower-intent volume, which is exactly the outcome you are trying to avoid.
How long before anything moves?
Paid channels can move inside the first month. Organic and local visibility are slower and depend on where you are starting. We set the expectation on the call using your baseline, not an industry average.
Do we have to switch our CRM or website?
Usually not. We instrument what you have first. If something genuinely blocks measurement we will show you what it is costing before recommending a change.
Who actually does the work?
The founders and the in-house team. Strategy is not handed to a junior after the sale, and there is no account manager layer relaying messages.
What if we are outside your three verticals?
We will refer you to someone who works in your category. Learning an unfamiliar industry on a client's budget is how both sides waste a year.
