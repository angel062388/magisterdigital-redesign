05 /   Questions

The things Houston operators ask before booking

What does this cost?
Engagements are scoped on the call against your close rate and average ticket, rather than from a rate card. Our current Houston retainer band is $20k to $60k per month, 90-day initial term, but if the numbers do not support the spend against well-funded competitors in a market that has drawn this much capital, we will say so on that call rather than three months in.
Do you guarantee a cost per lead?
No, and be careful with anyone who does before seeing your intake. A guaranteed cost per lead is easy to hit by buying cheaper, lower-intent volume, which is exactly the outcome you are trying to avoid.
How long before anything moves in Houston?
Paid channels can move inside the first month. Houston demand moves with the weather: storm and flood work spikes fast, and in this heat and humidity a flooded house starts growing mold within a day or two. The metro is also adding over a hundred thousand residents a year, so local visibility compounds. We set the timeline on the call from your baseline, not an industry average.
Do we have to switch our CRM or website?
Usually not. We instrument what you have first. If something genuinely blocks measurement we will show you what it is costing before recommending a change.
Do you have a Houston office?
No. Magister Digital is a national, operator-run team headquartered in San Diego. We do not have a Houston office, and we are not going to invent one. We are a remote partner with deep vertical knowledge, not a local storefront. The founders and the in-house team run every Houston engagement directly, with no account manager relaying messages and no local-office theater.
What if we are outside your three verticals?
We refer you to someone who works in your category. Learning an unfamiliar industry on a client's budget wastes a year for both sides.
