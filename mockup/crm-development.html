06 · Questions operators ask first

The questions a careful buyer is already holding

Do we have to switch CRM platforms?
No. We do not force you off your existing CRM. In most of the accounts we audit, the platform is fine and the configuration is the problem: generic stages, broken handoffs, automation nobody trusts. We rebuild on what you own.
Can you build from scratch if we have nothing?
Yes. If no system exists, we select the platform around how your team actually works, not around licensing cost. GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Clio, and custom builds are all in scope.
How does the CRM connect to our marketing?
The CRM is the loop-back for every channel we run. Paid media, SEO, and AI agents feed leads in with their source tag, and closed-won outcomes flow back to the channel that earned them. Attribution ends at revenue, not at clicks.
Who actually does the configuration?
Michael Merlino, Chief Strategist and AI Systems at Magister Digital and co-founder of Flowbots, owns the CRM work personally. It is not handed to junior staff once the engagement starts.
How do we know the data will hold after handoff?
Every automation is verified in staging before production, your team gets a recorded live training and a one-page operating reference, and a founder personally verifies the data for the first 30 days. Magister also runs TurnkeyRenovators, a multi-seven-figure construction company, on the same GoHighLevel stack it configures for clients.
What does CRM development cost?
Single-channel CRM engagements start at $20K per month, scoped after the audit based on platform complexity and how much attribution architecture gets built. Full-stack engagements start at $60K per month.
