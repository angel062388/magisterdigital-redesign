07 · Questions Houston operators ask first

The questions a careful Houston buyer is already holding

How long before organic moves in Houston?
Months, which is exactly why the work cannot start when the water does. Restoration pages built during a flood week are months too late for that flood. We set the timeline against your baseline on the call rather than an industry average.
Does Houston growth change the SEO case?
It strengthens it. A metro adding over a hundred thousand residents a year keeps producing people who have never heard of you, and organic visibility is the only channel where that audience arrives without you paying again each time.
What does an SEO engagement cost?
Single-channel SEO engagements start at $20K per month. Full-stack engagements, where SEO runs next to paid media, AI automation, CRM, and web, start at $60K per month. We scope from there based on market intensity. The real question is whether the work returns more than it costs, and we answer that on the call.
What tools do you actually use?
SEO Neo and Omega Indexer are built in-house through Stealth Code. SEO Neo handles AI citation monitoring, Omega Indexer handles indexing acceleration. We also run standard industry tools: Screaming Frog, Ahrefs, Search Console, and GA4. We do not rely on any one vendor's metric as the primary signal.
How do you handle AI search and citations?
Citability requires what ranks in traditional search: self-contained passages, named entities with schema backing, and content that survives extraction. We write to the passage-extraction standard from draft one. SEO Neo monitors which structures get cited in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude, and feeds that into every Houston brief.
Do you handle local SEO across Harris County?
Yes. Profile optimization, citation building, local schema and service-area architecture are part of the engagement, and in a footprint this wide the map pack usually decides more bookings than anything else we do.
What happens when Google updates its algorithm?
Sites built on genuine expertise, real entity footprints, and self-contained content tend to hold or improve through updates. Thin keyword-density sites drop. Magister Digital has run through every major update since 2008, with Infintech Designs as a standing proof. The six-dimension framework targets the intent behind updates, not the surface signal of any one cycle.
